## Competitor gap
Semrush shows Buffer, Hootsuite and Later winning broad Instagram demand: captions, bios, hashtags, Stories and Reels downloads. SocialFlow should not compete head-on for those terms yet. Start with lower-difficulty automation and approval-first workflow guides, then build free caption and hashtag resources that link to features.
